📖 Our Story

From our oven, with love

Fudgio started in a tiny home kitchen with one goal: the fudgiest brownie in town. Today we still bake every batch by hand — because you can taste the difference.

A secret recipe, perfected

Every Fudgio brownie is baked in small batches from our special, closely-guarded recipe. No preservatives, no factory lines — just premium chocolate, real butter, and a lot of patience. The result is a crackly top and an unbelievably gooey centre.

Three flavours we're proud of

  • Classic Chocolate — deep, dark and endlessly fudgy.
  • Nutty Delight — loaded with toasted nuts (contains nuts — not for those with a nut allergy).
  • Salted Caramel — sweet, salty and swirled with golden caramel.

Why Cash on Delivery?

We believe ordering dessert should be simple and trustworthy. That's why we offer Cash on Delivery only — you pay in cash when your fresh brownies arrive. No cards, no online payments, no worries.
